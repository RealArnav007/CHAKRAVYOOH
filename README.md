# CHAKRAVYOOH — Cyclone Intelligence & Resilient Disaster Operations Platform

An AI-powered multi-source satellite intelligence and offline-resilient emergency operations platform designed to identify, classify, track, and predict tropical cyclone patterns while orchestrating field disaster response.

## Overview
The system combines real-time data from Indian geostationary satellites (INSAT-3DR/3DS), polar scatterometry (EOS-06), coastal Doppler weather radars (Gopalpur, Paradip, Visakhapatnam), and oceanic buoy arrays (INCOIS/NIOT) with AI/ML physics-informed neural networks (PINN) and predictive modeling to transform raw Earth observations into actionable cyclone intelligence.

When cyclonic storms strike and conventional cellular towers/power grids fail, ordinary smartphones form a self-healing emergency mesh network: encrypted distress SOS messages hop peer-to-peer over Wi-Fi Aware and Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) until reaching an emergency gateway or command unit with uplink connectivity.

- **Satellite Geostationary & Scatterometer Telemetry**: Identify, predict, and monitor storm dynamics in real time.
- **AI & PINN Forecasting**: Multi-modal fusion tracking eye coordinates, central pressure, and sustained wind radiuses.
- **Dynamic 2D Risk Assessment Matrix**: Sector-level evacuation mandates across coastal vulnerable populations.
- **Chakravyooh Command Deck**: Unified web operations deck with officer authentication, 3D Earth globe visualization, and field dispatch coordination.
